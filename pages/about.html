Background & Philosophy

Designing at the boundary of intent and computation.

The Perspective

I believe the greatest software does not feel like software at all — it feels like an effortless extension of human intention.

Over the past 6+ years, I have worked across the entire interaction stack: from probabilistic generative AI canvases and high-density telemetry dashboards, to public infrastructure workflows and patient-centric healthcare systems.

My approach combines rigorous information architecture with typographic editorial discipline. I believe that an interface is not merely a coat of paint applied at the end of a sprint; it is the mental model of the product itself.
